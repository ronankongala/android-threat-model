App and Developer Information

 This Privacy Policy applies to the app "drip period & cycle tracker" (drip), developed and maintained by the drip team, a project of the Heart of Code e.V., a registered organization based at Mariannenplatz 2a, 10997 Berlin, Germany.

Introduction

 This Privacy Policy sets out how the iOS and Android app "drip period & cycle tracker" uses and protects your personal data that you generate and store within the app.

Data use

 drip respects and celebrates your privacy. We do not collect, retain, or share any usage data or personal information. There are no ads, no spyware, and no tracking. All menstrual health data is stored only on your device, under your control.

 This includes:

- settings

- menstrual cycle tracking data

 The data is used to display statistics and apply fertility awareness rules. This data cannot be accessed by other apps.

 If you wish to delete all your app data you can do so by navigating to Settings > Data > Delete app data. This can also be done by uninstalling the app.

 Please note that while drip only saves your data locally, 
 depending on your device settings an automatic cloud backup might still take place. 
 We recommend checking your general settings to prevent this from happening.

Permissions

 For using reminders in drip you need to allow push notifications. If you don't want to use reminders in drip you simply don't allow notifications for the app.

Transparency

 You can read through the source code of drip to ensure the given information is correct. The source code is like a recipe: It tells you how much and what kind of ingredients you need and how you prepare them to cook a tasty meal or program a funky app.
